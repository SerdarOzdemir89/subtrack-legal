Privacy Policy

Shelf for iOS · Effective 29 October 2026

Shelf is built so that your supplement records stay on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in the app.

What the app stores

Products, ingredients, amounts, prices, intake logs, notes and any photos you attach are saved in a local database on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. They are not uploaded to us. We cannot read them.

Label scanning

When you scan a supplement label or pick a photo, the text is recognised on your device with Apple's Vision framework. The image and the recognised text are not sent anywhere. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Notifications

Daily reminders and refill alerts are scheduled locally with Apple's UserNotifications framework. They are generated on your device; no push server is involved.

Purchases

Shelf Pro is available as an auto-renewable yearly subscription and as a one-time lifetime purchase, both processed by Apple. Apple provides the app with a transaction receipt so Pro features can be unlocked. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction.

Data we collect

None. The App Store privacy label for Shelf is "Data Not Collected".

Health disclaimer

Shelf adds up the numbers you enter. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose or treat anything, and it cannot see ingredients a label leaves out. Talk to a qualified professional before changing what you take.

Exporting and deleting

You can export a CSV or a one-page PDF at any time and share it wherever you choose. Deleting a product removes its ingredients and logs from the device. Settings → Delete all data removes everything.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
