Support

Shelf for iOS

Shelf keeps all of your data on your iPhone. There is no account to reset and no cloud copy we can look at.

Common questions

How do I add a supplement?
Cabinet → + → scan the label, or type the name, the bottle size and the nutrients per serving.

Why does the app ask for a bottle price?
It uses the price and the number of servings to show what your stack costs per month. You can leave it empty.

What does "over the upper limit" mean?
It compares the sum of what you entered with the adult tolerable upper intake level for that nutrient. It covers supplements only, not food.

How do I restore Shelf Pro?
Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that bought it.

How do I cancel the subscription?
iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Shelf. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I export my stack?
Settings → Export → Prepare CSV or Prepare PDF, then share it.

How do I delete everything?
Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app.
