Shelf

Supplement tracker for iPhone · 2026

Shelf turns the supplement labels on your counter into a real number: what you actually take every day, per nutrient, next to the adult upper limit. It scans the label on your device, adds up the nutrients across products, warns you when the same ingredient hides in three bottles, and shows what the whole stack costs per month.

No account. No cloud. No ads. Nothing for sale inside the app.

What it does

  • Scans supplement labels with Apple's Vision framework, on device.
  • Converts IU, mcg, mg and g into one comparable total.
  • Shows the daily total per nutrient and the share of the adult upper limit.
  • Flags duplicated ingredients across products.
  • Tracks how many servings are left and reminds you before a bottle runs out.
  • Calculates the monthly cost of your stack.
  • Exports a one-page PDF for your doctor and a CSV of everything.

Languages

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish (Spain and Mexico), Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Arabic, Hindi and Indonesian.
