Plant Pal for iPhone · 1 November 2026

Why does it tell me to check the soil instead of just watering?

Because a fixed schedule is what kills houseplants. The reminder names the pot, the light and the last watering, then asks you to check 3 cm down. That one habit prevents most root rot.

How accurate is the light meter?

It reads the camera's exposure and converts it to lux. It is an estimate: good enough to separate a dark corner from a bright windowsill, not a laboratory instrument. Hold the phone at the plant's height, screen facing the window, and let the number settle.

The unlock does not appear

Make sure the device is signed in to the App Store, then open Settings inside the app and tap Restore purchase. If it still does not appear, write to us with the Apple ID country and the date of purchase.

Why is there no plant identification?

Identification is a different product with a different cost, and the apps that do it pay for it with subscriptions. Plant Pal asks you to pick the species once — 28 common ones are included — and spends its effort on what happens after that.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a plant to remove its history, or delete the app to remove everything. There is no account and no server copy.

Something looks wrong in the advice

Send a photo and the plant's spot description. Advice bugs are treated as bugs, and they get fixed in the next update.
