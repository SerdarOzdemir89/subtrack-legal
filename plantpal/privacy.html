Privacy Policy

Plant Pal for iPhone · last updated 1 November 2026

Plant Pal is built so that there is nothing to collect. There is no account, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no server that stores your data.

What stays on your phone

Plants, spots, light readings, lawns, photos, care history and notes are stored in the app's own container on your device. They are included in your normal iPhone backup if you have iCloud Backup turned on; that backup is between you and Apple, not us.

The two exceptions, in full

FeatureWhat leaves the phoneTo whom
Frost forecast and lawn water balance The latitude and longitude of the city you typed. Nothing else — no identifier, no plant data. Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a free weather service, once a day at most.
Unlock purchase Handled entirely by Apple. We receive a receipt saying the unlock was bought; we never see your name, card or address. Apple (StoreKit).

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to estimate light. The app reads exposure values from the camera feed and never saves or uploads an image. Photos you add to a plant timeline stay in the app's local storage.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. There is no push server and no device token.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

Deleting a plant deletes its history. Deleting the app deletes everything the app stored. There is no account to close because there is no account.
