Plant Pal

Houseplant and lawn care for iPhone · 2026

Plant Pal does not sell you a schedule. It works out what your plant or lawn actually needs and tells you the reason: the light in that corner, the size of the pot, how long the soil stays wet, how cold it will get tonight, how much rain fell this week.

No subscription. One unlock, once. No account, no ads, no trackers.

What it does

  • Risk engine. Every plant gets a reason and an action: too wet, too dry, not enough light, cold damage, pests, crowded roots. It never says "roots are rotting" without evidence.
  • Light map. Measure each spot with the camera once. Plant Pal tells you which species will thrive there and which is slowly starving.
  • Frost watch. Per-species cold limits, with a penalty for pots, using tonight's forecast.
  • Lawn water balance. Rain and evapotranspiration over the last week, turned into millimetres and litres for your lawn.
  • Plant sitter sheet. One PDF a neighbour can follow while you are away.

The rules we keep

  • Free for three plants, forever. The unlock removes the limit.
  • Everything is stored on the phone. No account, no cloud sync.
  • No identification feature. We are not going to pretend a camera can name a plant better than a person can.
  • No plant shop, no affiliate links, nothing for sale inside the app.
