Privacy Policy

Twinfile for Mac · last updated 4 November 2026

Twinfile finds duplicate photos on your Mac. There is no account, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no server of ours that ever receives a photo.

What happens to your photos

Twinfile lists the folder you choose, reads a small thumbnail of each image to compute a 64-bit fingerprint, and compares those fingerprints on this Mac. Nothing is uploaded, no image is sent for processing, and the app works fully offline. Duplicates are moved into a folder inside the directory you scanned, never deleted outright.

The two exceptions, in full

FeatureWhat leaves your MacTo whom
Unlock purchase The purchase and the receipt. Handled entirely by Apple; we never see your name, card or address. Apple (StoreKit).
Opening the Privacy or Terms link A normal web request for this page, which reveals your IP address to GitHub Pages, the host. GitHub Pages — only if you click the link.

There is no third exception. No crash reporter, no usage counter, no "anonymous statistics".

The Photos library is out of scope

Twinfile deliberately does not open your Photos library. A .photoslibrary is a package, and the app skips packages entirely: Apple's own Photos app already offers a Duplicates album for that library, and reading someone's library behind their back is not a thing this app does.

What is stored on your Mac

  • The fingerprint of each scanned photo is kept in memory for the session only. Quitting the app discards it.
  • Your settings — the last sensitivity and whether the walkthrough was seen — live in the app's own preferences.
  • Quarantine manifests — a small JSON file next to the moved files, recording where each one came from so it can be put back.

Deleting the app removes the first two. Files you moved to quarantine stay where you put them, which is the point.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted to us, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, write to the address below and we will answer within 30 days.
