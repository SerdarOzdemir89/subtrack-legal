What the app does, and what it does not do

Twinfile compares images, using a perceptual fingerprint rather than a checksum or a file name. That means a copy which was resized, recompressed or renamed is usually found; it also means the match is a judgement, not a proof. Every group is presented for your review, one copy is marked as the keeper, and you can change that choice before anything moves.

Duplicates are moved into a dated quarantine folder inside the scanned directory, with a manifest. "Put Them Back" returns them to their original paths. Nothing is deleted until you press "Delete Permanently", and that action routes through the system Trash.

The app does not read your Photos library, does not touch files you did not point it at, and does not upload anything.

Your responsibility

You decide what to remove. Keep a backup of anything irreplaceable before deleting: no software should be the only copy of your photos. Sensitivity levels are a trade-off — the widest setting finds more, and may group photos that are merely similar.
