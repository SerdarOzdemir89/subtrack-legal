Twinfile

Point it at a folder. It finds the photos you own twice, shows you how many gigabytes that is, and moves the copies aside — keeping the best one.

Duplicate photo cleanup for macOS · on the Mac App Store

It compares pictures, not names

A resize, a re-compress, a rename, a copy that came back from a phone backup: a file-name search sees three different files. Twinfile reduces every photo to a 64-bit fingerprint and compares those, so the same picture matches even when nothing about the file matches.

What it does

  • Scans a folder, a drive or an old backup and groups copies by how the picture looks.
  • Picks the copy to keep using sharpness, resolution and file weight — and lets you overrule it with one click.
  • Names the price of the mess: “38.2 GB in duplicate photos”, before you pay anything.
  • Three sensitivity levels, from near-identical to “include re-crops and edits”.
  • Moves copies into a dated quarantine folder with a manifest; put them back with one click.

What it does not do

  • It does not upload anything. No server, no account, works offline.
  • It does not delete on the first click, and it does not touch the copy it keeps.
  • It does not read your Photos library — Apple's app already has a Duplicates album. Twinfile is for everything outside it.
  • It does not ask for a subscription. One purchase unlocks removal.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Scanning is local; a 50,000-photo drive takes a few minutes.
