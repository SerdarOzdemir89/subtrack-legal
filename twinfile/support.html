Common questions

Why does it not scan my Photos library?
A .photoslibrary is a package, and Twinfile skips packages. Apple's Photos app has its own Duplicates album for that library. Twinfile is for the other mess: old drives, imported folders, phone backups, downloads, exports.
Where did the removed photos go?
Into a folder named Twinfile Quarantine <date> inside the directory you scanned. Open it from the confirmation sheet, or press "Put Them Back" to return every file to its original path.
Is my "keep" choice permanent?
No. Click any thumbnail in a group before moving anything — the green "Keep" badge follows your click. Twinfile suggests, you decide.
Two photos that are not the same got grouped.
Set the sensitivity to "Near-identical only" and the list re-groups instantly without rescanning. The widest setting also matches re-crops and edited versions, which is useful for bursts and less useful for a tidy library.
Does it upload my photos?
No. Comparison runs on your Mac, offline. See the privacy policy.
How do I restore the purchase on another Mac?
Settings (⌘,) → Restore Purchases. The unlock is tied to your Apple account, not the machine.
