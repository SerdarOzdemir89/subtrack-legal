Subscriptions, reminders, and importing statements

Frequently asked

Does SubTrack cancel my subscriptions?

No. SubTrack never logs into a service or cancels anything on your behalf. It shows what you pay for, tells you before a renewal, and links you to the cancellation page for each service. This is deliberate: apps that claim to cancel subscriptions for you usually cannot, and that is the most common complaint in this category.

How does importing work?

Take a screenshot of your bank statement, your App Store subscriptions screen, or a receipt, then tap the scan button on the home screen. The text is read on your device and the charges it finds are listed for you to confirm. Nothing is added silently. If a charge is wrong, untick it before saving.

A reminder did not arrive

Open iOS Settings, then Notifications, then SubTrack, and check that notifications are allowed. Inside the app, Settings shows the current permission state and has a "Reschedule all reminders" button. Reminders are scheduled at 9:00 local time, the chosen number of days before the renewal date.

I bought Pro but it is not showing

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore purchases". If it still does not appear, make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I export my data?

Settings, then "Export as CSV". The file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet, so you choose where it goes. The app keeps no copy.
