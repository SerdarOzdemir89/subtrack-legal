Privacy Policy

SubTrack for iOS · Effective 27 October 2026

SubTrack is built so that your subscription data stays on your device. There is no account, no server, and no analytics SDK in the app.

What the app stores

The subscriptions you add or confirm are saved in a local database on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you have iCloud enabled for the app, that database syncs through your own private iCloud container, which Apple controls and we cannot read.

Statement scanning

When you import a screenshot or PDF, the text is read on your device with Apple's Vision framework. The image and the recognised text are not uploaded anywhere. The app keeps only the subscriptions you explicitly confirm.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or account identifier
  • No device identifier or advertising identifier
  • No usage analytics, crash reports, or third-party SDKs
  • No advertising, and no data sold or shared with anyone

Notifications

Renewal reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. They are generated on your device and are not sent through a push server.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple provides us with anonymised sales totals only. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Deleting your data

Delete a subscription inside the app and it is removed from the local database. Deleting the app removes everything it stored, including the local database. There is nothing on our side to delete because nothing is sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
