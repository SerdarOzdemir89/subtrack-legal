Privacy Policy

Proofsheet for iOS · Effective 5 November 2026

Proofsheet is built so that evidence stays with the person who captured it. There is no account, no server, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in the app.

What the app stores

Cases, notes, photos, coordinates, headings, timestamps, noise readings and the SHA-256 seals are saved as files inside the app's container on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us. We cannot read them.

Camera and photo library

When you capture a record, the camera image is written to the app's container. If you import a photo instead, only the item you choose is read and converted to JPEG. The app does not browse or upload your photo library.

Location

With your permission, Proofsheet asks Core Location for a position while you capture. The latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy and course are written into that record and into its seal. Location is never sent off the device. You can capture without location; the record is then sealed without coordinates and says so.

Microphone

The noise log uses the microphone to measure an approximate sound pressure level. Audio is processed in memory and reduced to numbers; it is not recorded to a file and is not uploaded. The reading is not a certified sound level meter.

Purchases

Proofsheet Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple provides the app with a signed transaction so Pro features can be unlocked. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment details. Apple's privacy policy governs the transaction.

Data we collect

None. The App Store privacy label for Proofsheet is "Data Not Collected".

Sharing and deleting

A PDF report or a record file leaves the app only when you choose to share it. Deleting a case removes its files from the app's container. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Settings → Delete all data removes every case and file.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
