What Proofsheet does

Proofsheet captures photos, location, time and a noise reading, seals each record with a SHA-256 hash that includes the previous record, and produces a PDF report. The seal is a mathematical check that the record and its file have not changed since capture. It is not a legal certification, a notarisation, or a guarantee that a court will admit the record.
