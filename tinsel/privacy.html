Tinsel — Privacy Policy

Tinsel: Christmas Games for Kids · Effective 12 November 2026

Short version: Tinsel collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK. Everything the app remembers stays on the device.

What the app stores

Tinsel saves the stars your child earns in the app's local preferences on this device. That record is used only to show progress on the home screen and the 24-door map. It is not uploaded, shared, or linked to a person.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or account identifier
  • No device identifier or advertising identifier
  • No usage analytics, crash reports, or third-party SDKs
  • No photos, contacts, location, microphone, or camera access
  • No advertising, and no data sold or shared with anyone

Children's privacy

Tinsel is made for young children and is designed to work without collecting personal information. The app has no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and no links that a child can reach without passing a parent gate. Because no personal information is collected, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Purchases

Tinsel is a one-time paid app. The purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Links

The parent section contains links to this policy and to the support page. Those links open in the device browser and are behind a simple parent gate.

Your choices, retention and deletion

We keep no copy of your data on a server, because there is none. What the app stores stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app, or when you use the in-app delete or reset option where one exists. We cannot retain what we never receive.

Because no personal data is collected, there is no consent to withdraw and no stored profile to request. If you believe we hold something about you, write to yasarserdarozdemir@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days, or delete it if it exists.

The only third parties involved are Apple, which processes purchases and supplies the system frameworks the app runs on, and, where the app has one, the weather service named above. Both publish privacy policies that give protection equal to this one. No data is shared with advertisers or data brokers, and nothing is sold.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
