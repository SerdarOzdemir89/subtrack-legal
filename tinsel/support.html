Tinsel — Support

Christmas games for little ones, ages 2–5

What is Tinsel?

Tinsel is a small collection of Christmas games for toddlers and preschoolers: matching pairs, shapes, counting, and decorating a tree. Four games, six levels each, 24 doors in total. No ads, no subscriptions, no account.

How do I play?

Tap Play on the home screen to pick a game, or tap 24 doors to choose any level from the map. Every level is available; nothing is locked behind a timer or a purchase.

Is there sound?

Yes. Sound effects can be turned off in the parent section: tap For grown-ups on the home screen, solve the sum, then use the Sound switch.

How do I reset progress?

Open For grown-ups, solve the sum, then tap Reset progress. This clears the stars on this device and cannot be undone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Tinsel has no server and no network features. It works in airplane mode.

Is my child's data collected?

No. Tinsel collects no personal information, has no analytics, and shows no advertising. See the privacy policy.
