Privacy Policy

Shrinkray for Mac · last updated 4 November 2026

Shrinkray compresses images on your Mac. There is no account, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no server of ours that ever receives your files.

What happens to your images

You choose the files, Shrinkray reads them, re-encodes them with Apple's ImageIO framework, and writes the result to the folder you picked. The pixels never leave the machine: there is no upload step, no cloud conversion and no queue that waits for a network connection. The app works fully offline.

The two exceptions, in full

FeatureWhat leaves your MacTo whom
Unlock purchase The purchase and the receipt. Handled entirely by Apple; we never see your name, card or address. Apple (StoreKit).
Opening the Privacy or Terms link A normal web request for this page, which reveals your IP address to GitHub Pages, the host. GitHub Pages — only if you tap the link.

There is no third exception. No crash reporter, no usage counter, no "anonymous statistics".

What is stored on your Mac

  • Your output folder. macOS gives the app a security-scoped bookmark so it can keep writing to the folder you chose after a relaunch. It is a file reference, not a copy of your data, and it stays inside the app's own sandbox container.
  • Your settings. The last preset and options you used, kept in the app's preferences.

Deleting the app removes both. Your images are never copied into the app container: the compressed copies are written only to the folder you selected.

Metadata

Compressed files are written fresh, so EXIF data — camera model, GPS coordinates, timestamps — is dropped in the process. That is deliberate: a photo prepared for a marketplace listing should not carry the coordinates of your home.

Children

Shrinkray does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted to us, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, write to the address below and we will answer within 30 days.
