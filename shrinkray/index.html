Shrinkray

Drop a folder of images. Get files that fit the size limit — at the highest quality that still fits it.

Image compressor for macOS · on the Mac App Store

The limit is the feature

Most compressors give you a quality slider and let you guess. Shrinkray asks for the number that actually matters — 500 KB, 200 KB, 5 MB — and searches for the highest quality that lands under it, then tells you which quality it used. If the limit cannot be met without destroying the picture, it shrinks the pixel size instead of lying to you.

What it does

  • Batch compression for JPEG, PNG, HEIC and TIFF.
  • Presets that match a destination, not a mood: marketplace listing, social post, email attachment, web page.
  • Longest-edge resizing, so a 6000 px photo becomes 2000 px without touching the aspect ratio.
  • EXIF and GPS stripped, because a listing photo should not carry the coordinates of your home.
  • A running total: 24.2 MB in, 2.5 MB out, 89% smaller — for the batch, not per file guesswork.
  • Menu bar item that compresses whatever image is on the clipboard.

What it does not do

  • It does not upload anything. There is no server; the app works offline.
  • It does not overwrite your originals.
  • It does not ask for a subscription. One purchase unlocks everything.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. About 4 MB of disk space.
