Write to yasarserdarozdemir@gmail.com. Include your macOS version (Apple menu → About This Mac) and, if a file misbehaved, what format it was in. A human answers, usually within two working days.

Common questions

The compressed file is not as small as I expected.
A size limit is a ceiling, not a target: the engine keeps the highest quality that still fits under it. If the source is already small — a screenshot, a line drawing — there may be nothing to win, and the row says "no change" instead of writing a heavier copy.
Where do the compressed files go?
To the folder shown under "Save to" in the sidebar, with a suffix appended to the name (photo.jpg becomes photo-listing.jpg). Your originals are never modified. Right-click a finished row → Show in Finder.
Why does the app ask for a folder?
macOS sandboxes every App Store app. Shrinkray can read the files you hand it and write to one folder you pick; it cannot see the rest of your disk. The choice is remembered across launches.
Does it upload my images?
No. Everything runs on your Mac, offline. See the privacy policy.
How do I restore my purchase on a new Mac?
Open Shrinkray → Settings (⌘,) → Restore Purchases. The unlock is tied to your Apple account, not to the machine.
Why is a PNG bigger after "compressing"?
PNG has no quality setting, so a size limit is met by reducing pixels; when even that does not help, the engine leaves your original untouched rather than hand back a heavier file.

Keyboard

⌘O add images · ⇧⌘O change output folder · ⌘, settings. The menu bar icon compresses whatever image is on the clipboard with your current preset.
