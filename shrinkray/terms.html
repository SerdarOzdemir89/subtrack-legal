What the app does and does not do

Shrinkray re-encodes images and, when you set a size limit, searches for the highest quality that fits it. It never overwrites your originals: output files carry a suffix and land in the folder you choose. When a file cannot be made smaller, the original is left untouched and the row says so.

Quality settings and size limits are engineering trade-offs, not guarantees of visual quality. Review your compressed files before you upload them anywhere that matters — a marketplace listing, a print lab, a client deliverable.
