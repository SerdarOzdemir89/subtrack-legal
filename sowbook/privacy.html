Privacy Policy

Sowbook for iOS · Effective 6 November 2026

Sowbook is a garden planner that works on your device. There is no account, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in the app. The only network request is the optional weather forecast described below.

What the app stores

Your gardens, beds, plantings, harvests and seed lots are saved in the app's own database on your iPhone or iPad. They are not uploaded to us. We cannot read them.

Permissions

Sowbook asks for no camera, microphone, contacts or photo-library access. Photos in the journal are picked through the system file picker, which does not grant the app access to your library.

Location is optional and used for one thing only: the weather card. If you never turn on "Use my location", the forecast uses the centre of the frost region you picked, and iOS never asks for permission. If you do turn it on, the app sends the coordinate to Open-Meteo with the forecast request and keeps it only in memory. It is not stored in the database and not sent anywhere else.

Network

The garden planner itself works offline. The weather card calls Open-Meteo, a public forecast service that needs no account or API key, with a latitude and longitude. The request carries no name, no device identifier and no advertising identifier, and Open-Meteo's own privacy policy governs what its servers log. Without a network connection the card shows "Forecast unavailable" and every other screen keeps working.

Exporting a CSV or PDF creates a file on your device and leaves the app only when you choose to share it through the system share sheet.

Purchases

Sowbook Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple provides the app with a signed transaction so Pro features can be unlocked. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's privacy policy governs the transaction.

Data we collect

We collect no data about you. We have no server and no way to identify a user. The App Store privacy label for Sowbook is "Data Not Collected". The one third-party request is the weather lookup: with your permission the coordinate goes to Open-Meteo to return a forecast and is not retained by us.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes every garden, bed, planting, harvest and seed lot from the app's database. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
