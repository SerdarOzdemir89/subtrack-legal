Sowbook · iOS

A vegetable garden planned from your frost dates.

Sowbook turns two dates — the last frost of spring and the first frost of autumn — into a week-by-week plan: what to start indoors, what to move outside, what to pick, and when to sow the next row.

What is inside
  • 32 crops with real spacing, days to maturity and frost hardness
  • Square-foot bed plans with rotation warnings
  • Succession sowing so a row keeps coming
  • Harvest log and seed stash
  • PDF and CSV export
  • No account, no server, no subscription required to use the free garden

Sowbook Pro unlocks unlimited gardens and beds, the full catalogue, succession and rotation tools, records and export. One yearly price or one lifetime purchase, sold through the App Store.
