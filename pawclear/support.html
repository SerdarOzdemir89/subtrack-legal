Common questions

Where are my records stored?
On your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes the records, so export anything you need first.

Why does PawClear say a vaccination is invalid?
The most common reasons are a microchip implanted after the rabies vaccine, a vaccine given before 12 weeks of age, or a primary dose still inside its waiting period. Open the requirement to see the exact reason.

How is the earliest travel date calculated?
Only requirements that take time to satisfy move the date: age limits, the rabies waiting period after a primary dose, and titer timing. Certificates and treatments can be completed closer to departure.

What is a facility QR?
A kennel, groomer or daycare can create its own requirement profile in the app and print its QR code. Scanning it loads those exact windows instead of guessing.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open PawClear Pro and tap "Restore purchase". Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

Is the PDF an official document?
No. It is a record summary you can show to a kennel, groomer or vet. Official travel documents must be issued or endorsed by the competent authority.
