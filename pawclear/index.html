What it does

  • Checks each requirement against its own validity window: kennel, daycare, groomer, airline, border.
  • Calculates the earliest legal travel date, including the rabies waiting period, titer timing and age limits.
  • Produces one A4 record card with a QR code for a kennel desk or a vet.
  • Loads a facility's exact rules by scanning the QR code it prints at the front desk.

Details

PawClear keeps records and tracks requirements. It does not diagnose or treat. The authority, airline, veterinarian or facility has the final say. The PDF is a record summary, not an official health certificate or pet passport.
