Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

PawClear keeps pet records and checks travel or facility requirements. It is built so that your records stay yours.

What we collect

Nothing. PawClear has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracker. We do not receive your pet records, photos, documents or usage data.

Where your data lives

Records are stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData. If you enable iCloud sync in a future version, data will be stored in your own private iCloud account under Apple's terms; we cannot read it. Deleting the app deletes its local data.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a vaccine card or a facility QR code. Images are processed on the device with Apple's Vision framework. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Apple processes the payment; we receive only the information Apple provides to every developer (such as whether a subscription is active), never your card details.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

Children

PawClear is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your choices

You can export or delete your records from the app at any time. Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Medical disclaimer

PawClear keeps records and tracks requirements. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Requirements are provided for information; the authority, airline, veterinarian or facility has the final say.
