Kobold — Support

A solo tabletop adventure that runs on your phone

What is Kobold?

Kobold is a single-player tabletop role-playing game. A deterministic rules engine owns the dice, the combat and the inventory; the narration is written on the device. There is no account and no server.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Kobold has no network features. It works in airplane mode. Only App Store purchases need a connection.

The narration text is missing or plain

On devices without Apple Intelligence, Kobold still plays the full adventure with written scene text and the rules engine driving every outcome. Nothing is locked behind the narrator.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the in-app store and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to a Kobold account.

Is my data collected?

No. Kobold collects no personal information, has no analytics and shows no advertising. See the privacy policy.
