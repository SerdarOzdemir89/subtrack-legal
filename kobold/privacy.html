Kobold — Privacy Policy

Kobold: Solo RPG · Effective 16 November 2026

Short version: Kobold collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. Nothing you type or play leaves the device.

What the app does with your text

Kobold lets you type free-text actions during an adventure. That text is read by the app's rules engine on the device. On supported devices the narration is written by Apple Intelligence through Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework; the text is not sent to us or to any other server, and there is no network request in the app other than StoreKit.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or account identifier
  • No device identifier or advertising identifier
  • No usage analytics, crash reports, or third-party SDKs
  • No location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts access
  • No advertising, and no data sold or shared with anyone

Purchases

Kobold is free to download with one full adventure. The Pro unlock and the adventure packs are in-app purchases processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Children

Kobold is rated 12+ for fantasy violence and mild language. It is not directed at children under 13, contains no chat and no user-generated content, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices, retention and deletion

We keep no copy of your data on a server, because there is none. What the app stores stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app, or when you use the in-app delete or reset option where one exists. We cannot retain what we never receive.

Because no personal data is collected, there is no consent to withdraw and no stored profile to request. If you believe we hold something about you, write to yasarserdarozdemir@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days, or delete it if it exists.

Apple processes purchases and, on supported devices, the system frameworks the app relies on; Apple's privacy policy applies to both and gives protection equal to this policy. No data is shared with advertisers or data brokers, and nothing is sold. StoreKit purchase receipts are handled by Apple so the app can unlock what you bought.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
