N Nestbook

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Nestbook is a pregnancy and newborn record. It is built so that there is nothing to collect: no account, no server, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no third-party library.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not receive your records, your baby's name, photos, dates or usage data. There is no sign-up and therefore no account to delete.

Where your data lives

Feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, growth measurements, symptoms, appointments, vaccines, first foods, recovery notes and photos are stored in the app's own database on your device (Apple SwiftData). If you use iCloud Backup, that backup is between you and Apple; we cannot read it. Deleting the app deletes its local data. Nestbook writes a daily backup file inside its own folder on the device and keeps the last seven; you can restore or share them from Settings.

The only things that leave the app's own database

FeatureWhat is sharedWhere it goes
Home-screen widget and Live Activity The baby's first name, the last feed time and the last diaper time An App Group container on this device, readable only by Nestbook and its widget. Nothing is uploaded.
Doctor summary (PDF), handoff card and QR, CSV, backup file Whatever you choose to share, generated on the device Only to the destination you pick in the iOS share sheet
Purchases Handled entirely by Apple Apple. We do not receive your name, payment method or address.

Photos

Photos are chosen with the system photo picker and stored inside the app's database after being resized. Nestbook does not have access to your photo library beyond the images you pick, and nothing is uploaded.

Notifications

Reminders (daily check-in, morning summary, vaccine dates, first-food watch windows) are scheduled locally with Apple's notification framework. They are generated on the device and are not sent through a server.

Health data, location, contacts

Nestbook does not read or write Apple Health data, does not request location, and does not access contacts. Vaccine schedules are reference text shipped with the app.

Children

Nestbook is used by parents and caregivers. It is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to migrate or delete on our side.
