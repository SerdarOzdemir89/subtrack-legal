Do I need an account?

No. Nestbook has no account, no login and no server. Everything is stored on your device.

How do I move to a new phone?

Settings → Your data → Back up everything, then send the file to the new phone (AirDrop, Files, email to yourself). On the new phone, install Nestbook and choose I already have a backup on the first screen, or Settings → Your data → Restore from a backup. The app also writes one backup a day on the device and keeps the last seven.

My widget shows "Open Nestbook"

The widget reads only two lines written by the app when it opens. Open Nestbook once after installing, log or view something, and the widget fills in within a few minutes.

Why is the feed timer not on the lock screen?

Live Activities must be allowed for Nestbook: iOS Settings → Nestbook → Live Activities. Starting a feed timer in the app adds it to the lock screen and the Dynamic Island.

Is the dose number a prescription?

No. It is arithmetic on the package insert's mg/kg band for the weight you typed, converted to the bottle's concentration. If the baby is below the label's minimum age, the app hides the number and tells you to call a clinician. Always check the bottle and the label.

Are the vaccine dates official?

They are a reference schedule for Türkiye (T.C. Sağlık Bakanlığı) and the United States (CDC), counted from the birth date. Each screen links to the official source. Your clinician decides what is given and when.

What does Pro include?

WHO percentile bands on the growth chart, the one-page doctor PDF, CSV export and backup/restore, and the first-food (allergen) plan. Everything needed at 3 a.m. — logging, the kick counter, the contraction timer, week-by-week notes and the night mode — is free and unlimited.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app: its database and photos go with it. Backups you exported stay wherever you put them, so delete those too if you want them gone.
