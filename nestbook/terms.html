What Nestbook is

Nestbook is an educational record for pregnancy and the first years. It helps you write down what happened, show a summary to a clinician, and see measurements against published growth standards.

What Nestbook is not

It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, monitor or predict any condition. Dose arithmetic follows the package insert's mg/kg band and hides its result below the label's minimum age; it is not a prescription. Vaccine schedules are reference text — your health authority and your clinician decide what is given and when. The contraction pattern alert is a timing aid, not advice about when to go to hospital. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
